Task sheet — phone

Tap any task anywhere (Today, Inbox, a calendar block, search) and this one sheet opens over the page you were on. Medium first, drag up for full height; swipe down, the scrim or Back closes it and keeps every edit. Built only from the Mobile Kit: MK Bottom Sheet, chips, MK Action Sheet, pickers, MK Undo Toast, MK States.
