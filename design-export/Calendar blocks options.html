How should the calendar's blocks look?

Four directions for the blocks, drawn on the same calendar: yesterday (past), today (with the now line), and two more days, at the app's default 125% size, by day and in Night Garden, and one column at 150%. Then three ways to lay out blocks that overlap, and three looks for the goal of the day. Answer with letters, for example "blocks B, overlap A, goal C". Colours are the app's own tokens; none is changed.

What Akiflow, Notion Calendar and Amie do

  • Akiflow: a block is a rounded rectangle tinted in its project's colour with a solid bar of that colour down the left; a bold title with the time under it; a task carries a small square check before its title; a 15–30 minute block shrinks to one line (title, then the time); done and past blocks fade, done titles are struck. Overlapping blocks sit side by side. A Time Slot is a project-coloured box with its name, a task count and a completion bar along the bottom, its tasks listed inside.
  • Notion Calendar: soft tinted fills from an eight-colour palette, small 4–6px corners, no heavy edges, hairline grid; the title, then the time in grey; past events fade; overlaps side by side.
  • Amie: playful, rounder pastel blocks; to-dos dragged onto the calendar become blocks with a round check; done ones strike through.

Blocks

Every direction shows: a task with its check and an event · a Time slot holding two tasks · the goal of the day · done and past · "Now · 51m left" and "Ran over" · 15, 30, 60 and 105 minutes with a long title · two and three overlapping blocks · the pointer over a block (its actions) · two blocks selected (for the new multi-select).
