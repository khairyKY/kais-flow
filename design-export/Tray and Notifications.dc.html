The app,
outside the app

One K everywhere: the Source Serif K on paper where colour is allowed, a heavier white silhouette where it isn’t. Every notification is useful in one tap, and most are silent. System surfaces (Windows, Android, Chrome) keep their native type; our own surfaces (the tray flyout, Settings, history) use the journal kit. New glyphs: kf-bell · kf-moon · kf-focus-ring.
