D1

Two kinds of surface: tools and pages

Pages: the goal of the day; journal entries, notes and quotes; the herbarium. Everything else (lists, calendar, sheets, rituals, settings) is a tool.

Was: every card was a placed paper card.
D2

Colour stays exactly as it was

Every paper, ink, line and accent keeps its old value, day and Night Garden. Added only: a page paper (#FBF5E6 · night #2C2740), and selection filled with ink instead of a second accent.

Was: no separate page paper; selection used accent washes.
D3

Tools are rounded, pages stay near-square

Tool cards 16px · rows and inputs 12px · calendar blocks 12px · sheets 20px · checkbox 7px · buttons and chips pill. Pages 6px.

Was: 3px on every card (“this isn't a rounded UI”).
D4

Tools are flat; only pages cast a shadow

A tool reads by tone and a hairline. Pages keep a soft shadow. Floating layers (popovers, sheets, toasts) keep theirs.

Was: every card had the two-part warm shadow.
D5

Metadata in Inter Tight, sentence case

“Overdue 2d · 1h 30m” at 12px (12.5 on a phone), untracked. Courier Prime survives as the date stamp on pages. At most one uppercase eyebrow per screen.

Was: “OVERDUE 2D · 1H30M” in Courier Prime, uppercase, tracked 0.12–0.22em, 9.5–10.5px.
D6

Section labels: 14px semibold, sentence case

“Top 3”, “Up next”, “To plan”, with no dashed rule running after them.

Was: “TOP 3 - - - - - -” in tracked mono caps with a dashed rule.
D7

Tape, tilt, grain and handwriting live on pages only

The washi recipe is unchanged; it just never lands on a tool. The active sidebar item loses its tape marker; rail cards stop tilting; the app canvas has no grain (Settings' texture slider now sets the page grain).

Was: tape on the active nav item and placed cards, grain over the whole app.
D8

Solid hairlines in tools

Dividers between rows and sections are solid. Dashed stays for open time slots on the calendar and for ruling on pages.

Was: dashed rules everywhere, “like a ruled notebook”.
D9

Same terra, same place; no drop shadow. Tab icons are Kai's Flow's own; the active tab sits on a soft pill.

Was: 56px with a terra drop shadow.

Full window, no page margins; one header row (view · ‹ Today › on the left, date and daisy at the far right); a compact To plan list (one line per task, Plan on hover); half-hour rows 36px; under the grid, each day's tasks with no time yet; three blocks at the same time sit in equal thirds.

The recipe (unchanged)
  • Tint — the page's accent at 0.40–0.45; the paper reads through.
  • Weave — 4px white stripes at 0.32 on an 8px repeat.
  • Edge — 1px radius, a torn edge, not a chip.
  • Skew — 2–4°; two strips lean opposite ways.
  • Sizes — standard 52 × 15, wide 60 × 13, feature 82 × 19 (goal only). The 30 × 9 nav marker is retired (D7).
Tape it
  • The goal of the day, journal entries, notes, quotes, herbarium specimens.
  • At most two strips per page, three taped pages per screen.
Never
  • On any tool: rows, lists, nav, inputs, buttons, toasts, calendar blocks, rails.
  • As meaning — tape is never a status or a colour code.

Spacing & motion 07 · unchanged

4px base (8 · 16 · 24 · 40 · 64). Transform and opacity only: press 100ms + 0.97, chips 150ms, overlays 200ms, panels 300ms, sheets in 300 / out 200 on the emphasized curves, the bloom 600ms. At most two continuous animations per screen; all of it respects reduced motion.

House rules 08 · updated for B

Always
  • Semantic tokens only — never a hex literal in a component.
  • Know which surface you're on: a tool is flat and rounded; a page is paper.
  • One terra action and at most one gold goal page per view.
  • Each surface keeps its own flower and accent.
  • Metadata in Inter Tight, sentence case; the typewriter only stamps a page's date.
  • 48 × 48 targets; every gesture has a tap path (⋯). Delete is instant: Trash + Undo.
Never
  • Pure white, hard black, bright saturated primaries — or a new colour.
  • Tape, tilt, grain or handwriting on a tool. A shadow on a resting tool.
  • Uppercase tracked metadata; more than one eyebrow per screen.
  • A stage that contradicts the data — no full bloom on a full inbox.
  • Anything only reachable on hover on a phone. Anything under 12px on a phone.
  • Sukoon's components. Borrow the craft, never the parts.
