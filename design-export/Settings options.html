How should Settings be grouped and laid out?

Today Settings is two columns of cards plus a separate Integrations page. Below, every setting is sorted into 11 categories (the table), then drawn three ways: two panes, a cards home, or one page with an index. Each layout is shown on a 1280 computer at 100% and at 150%, by day and in Night Garden, and on a 390 phone, with real labels and controls. Layout

One list of settings, drawn three ways. Each layout shows a computer at 100% by day and in Night Garden, the same computer at 150% (the window is the same, the app is zoomed as it zooms), and the phone. The rows are drawn in your rows pick (A until you pick one); switch here to see them in B.

Rows and controls

Two polish passes for the rows themselves, drawn on the same three categories: Capture & AI (the new AI switches), Appearance (a five-way choice and a slider), and Account (the danger zone). Both work with any layout.

Search

Search sits at the top of the category list (A), the cards home (B) or the index (C), with Ctrl F to reach it. Typing swaps the settings for results, grouped by category. Each result has its real control, so you change it right there, and › opens its category with the row lit. Words match what they mean too: "dark" finds Theme, "delete" finds Delete account and Trash, "AI" finds the Capture & AI rows and AI assistants.

Notes

  • Icons: six categories reuse the kf line set: calendar, inbox (Capture & AI), bell, label (Organize), link (Integrations) and lock. Five are new, drawn on the same pen (24 grid, stroke 1.6, round): person (Account), half-moon disc (Appearance), cylinder (Your data), keyboard (Shortcuts) and info (App & updates). The Plan drawer's box stays only for Someday, so Your data doesn't share it.
  • Stays its own page: the importer (/settings/import) and Trash. Each provider in Integrations (Google, GitHub, AI assistants, capture key) opens its own page from its row, with today's controls on it.
  • Shows in two places: Google Calendar is a link row in Calendar & time, and its controls stay in Integrations. "What AI assistants can see" in Privacy & terms links to the same switches.
  • Only where it applies: "On this computer" shows in the Windows app only, "On this phone" in the Android app only, and "This device" (push) in a browser only, as today.
  • Kept: every row saves as you touch it. Copy, help lines and defaults are today's, or the builders' own.
