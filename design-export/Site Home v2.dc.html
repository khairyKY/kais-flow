Motion spec
PinningThe section pins for 5 × 100vh. One beat per viewport of scroll.
Per beat600ms, ease-out (cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)). Only transform + opacity.
PhoneThe outgoing screen fades to 0 and lifts 12px; the incoming one rises from +16px with opacity 0 → 1, overlapping by 200ms.
TextTitle and line cross-fade, 8px slide. The beat list dot moves with them.
LightThe background wash cross-fades between beats (an opacity layer, not a colour animation). Beat 5 switches to the night palette.
ArtThe flower on the right swaps with a 400ms fade, slight rotate (±3°).
Reduced motionNo pinning. The five beats stack as plain sections, each a phone beside its text, in this same order.
Phone layoutSame beats; the progress becomes five hairlines under the header.
