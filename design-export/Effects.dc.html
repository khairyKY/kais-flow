1Effects — the motion shelf. Ten reusable botanical effects, each a pure-CSS recipe.

Catalog only — nothing here is applied to existing screens. Each card is a living demo plus its recipe (keyframes name · duration · when to fire). All of them bow to prefers-reduced-motion: particles simply don't spawn, glows hold at their mid-state. First consumers: the Focus timer & Garden view.
