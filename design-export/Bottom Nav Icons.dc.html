Bottom nav icons

Five slots, four of them still placeholder dots in the shipping build. Three ways to fill them — each one takes a different position on how much botany belongs in a tab bar. All rendered at true 390pt width.

The slots

Order is fixed and shouldn't change: Today is the home a person lands on, Calendar and Tasks flank the capture button, More holds people, projects and settings. The centre is the voice-capture FAB and is not a tab — it never takes an active state.

Three directions
A Line at rest, bloom when active

The hybrid the build already gestures at, made a rule: every tab is a stroked line icon, and the selected one swaps to its botanical mark in the surface accent. The bar stays quiet; one flower blooms where you are.

Works because

Active state is unmistakable without a pill, underline or filled background — nothing else in the bar is ever coloured. Rest icons stay legible at 21px.

Costs

Two shapes per tab to draw and animate. The Today/More slots have no natural flower — they'd need one assigned.

B All line, tape marks the place

One family throughout — the section-08 line set, nothing else. The active tab is called out by a short washi strip above it in the surface accent, the same device that marks the active sidebar item on desktop.

Works because

One icon family, one metaphor, one thing to draw. The tape ties mobile to desktop, and it slides between tabs beautifully.

Costs

Breaks the house rule that tape only goes on placed cards. Least distinctive of the three — could be any app's tab bar.

C A pressed row of specimens

No line icons at all. Each tab is its own species, drawn once: dried and colourless at rest, full-colour where you stand. The bar becomes the herbarium strip at the bottom of the page.

Works because

Nothing else looks like it. Every tab already owns a species elsewhere in the product, so the bar teaches the colour system by using it.

Costs

Five near-identical rosettes at 21px are hard to tell apart by shape — it leans almost entirely on colour and the label. Fails an accessibility read.

Recommendation

A — line at rest, bloom when active. It's the only one that keeps the tab bar legible at a glance and unmistakably this product, and it's already half-built. C is the most beautiful and the least usable; B is the safe fallback if the two-shape swap proves too costly to animate.

To ship A, decide two things
  • Today's species. Sage is taken by Tasks. Suggest gold — it's the goal colour, and Today is where the goal lives.
  • More has no species. Keep the three dots stroked even when active, tinted --ink-body. A settings drawer shouldn't get a flower.
Shared rules, whichever wins
  • Icons at 21px, tap target 56 × 48 minimum.
  • Labels always visible — 8px mono, uppercase, 0.1em. Never icon-only.
  • Rest --ink-faint; active takes the surface accent. No pills, no filled backgrounds.
  • The FAB never changes state, never takes a badge, and keeps its 24px overhang.
  • Transition 180ms ease-out. Nothing bounces.
