Kai's Flow · Mobile Kit · Android · Sep 2026

The field journal,
in one hand

Every phone piece at 390 × 844, day above night, sizes beside it — pick one in the bar below (one piece is live at a time). Same paper, same species, same four fonts as desktop — just sized for a thumb: 48px targets, nothing under 12px, no hover anywhere. Every gesture has a tap path through ⋯. Android Back closes the top layer first. Tokens: ds/tokens/* · glyphs: Icons · states: Design System §04.

One Today-like screen built only from the pieces below: top bar, workload line, week strip, NOW card, section label, task rows (overdue, Top 3, subtasks, long title), undo toast and tab bar. It proves the kit — it is not the Today design.
