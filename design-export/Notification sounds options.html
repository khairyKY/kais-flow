Which sound for each notification?

Two or three short sounds for each kind of notification, between a third of a second and 1.2 seconds, in the app's two voices: felt (the muted felt piano you picked for the app's sounds) and glass (soft glass bells). All in one key, so two notifications in a row sound related. Each is levelled to about the same loudness, so compare them at one volume. Answer with letters, for example "task reminder A, event starting C, …", or pick below and copy.

Where each sound can play

  • Android app: a notification's sound belongs to its channel, set once when the channel is made; the app can't change it afterwards (only you can, in Android Settings). So each kind with its own sound gets its own channel with a WAV from the app's res/raw, and the channels made before (Reminders, Rituals, Focus, Quiet) are replaced by new ones once. Android also lets you swap any channel's sound yourself.
  • Windows app: a toast can only play Windows' own sounds (ms-winsoundevent:…) or a file inside an installed package (the Store install). For the installer build, the toast goes out silent and the app plays the WAV itself; it is running whenever it shows a toast, so that always works.
  • Web / installed web app: browsers ignore a custom notification sound; a push plays the system's own sound. When a window of the app is open, it can play the chosen sound itself.
  • Quiet hours and Pause still silence everything, whichever sounds you pick. The morning digest is silent today; pick one only if you want it heard.
