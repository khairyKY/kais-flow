Notification sound packs

Every sound from the options, kept, in three packs. Each pack has a sound for all ten kinds of notification, between half a second and 1.2 seconds, all in one key and levelled to about the same loudness. Felt and Glass are the A and B options in each voice; Mixed is every C option, both voices together, with a break end and a terms sound made for it. Pick a pack in the app: Settings → Notifications & sound → Notification sounds, where ▶ plays each one.

Where each sound can play

  • Android app: a notification's sound belongs to its channel, fixed once the channel is made. So each sound of your pack is its own channel ("Task reminder (Felt)") playing a WAV from the app's res/raw. Switching packs moves what's planned onto the new pack's channels and removes the old ones. Android also lets you swap any channel's sound yourself.
  • Windows app: the toast goes out silent and the app plays the pack's WAV itself. If it can't (it hasn't been clicked since it started), the toast keeps Windows' own sound.
  • Web / installed web app: browsers ignore a custom notification sound. When a window of the app is on screen it plays the pack's sound and the notification is silent; otherwise the browser's own sound plays.
  • Quiet hours and Pause still silence everything, whichever pack. The morning digest is silent today. Break end is in every pack, but the app doesn't send a break-end notification yet.
