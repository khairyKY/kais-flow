Guides › Installing

Install on Android

4 min · updated 3 Oct 2026

Kai’s Flow isn’t on the Play Store yet, so you install it yourself. It takes about a minute, and you only do the “allow” step once.

Before you start

You need Android 9 or newer and about 60 MB free.

1Download the APK

On your phone, open kaisflow.app/download and tap Download APK. It’s about 18 MB.

kaisflow-1.0.16.apk
18.2 MB · Download complete
Open

2Allow this one source

Android asks once whether your browser may install apps. Turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

Install unknown apps
Allow from this source

3Install and sign in

Tap Install, then Open. Sign in with the same account you use on the web; your days are already there.

KKai’s Flow
CancelInstall
Updating: the app tells you when there’s a new version. Tap Update, and the same three steps run on their own.

Press kit

Everything you need to write about it

Kai’s Flow is a free personal planner that looks and feels like a 19th-century botanical field journal. It brings tasks, time blocks, habits and a journal into one calm page, and works on the web, Android and Windows. It’s made by one student, Kai, who built it for himself first.
