3The block — five layered axes · 54px/hour, as shipped

Every real block is a composition: Kind owns hue · Semantic owns edge + one badge + fill pattern · Temporal owns opacity/saturation · Layout owns geometry · Interaction owns the overlay (outline, elevation, cursor) and nothing underneath. Precedence when two collide: Interaction > Semantic > Temporal > Kind. One badge slot, ranked conflict > cancelled > declined > tentative > needs-action > recurring > free — the losers fall back to the edge channel or go unmarked.

Base block, verbatim from the app: radius 6 · padding 7px 10px · fill accent 24% · border 1px accent-deep 35% (full, no spine) · title 12px · time 8.5px mono · no shadow at rest. Grid rhythm 54px = 1 hour, snap 27px = 30 min.

3aInteraction — overlay channel only: outline, elevation, cursor
Sheet 1 · interaction · same hue throughout

The hand on the block

Nothing here recolours the fill or the hue — an interaction must never look like a status change. Dragging and resizing are pairs of elements: a free ghost plus a snapping grid element.
