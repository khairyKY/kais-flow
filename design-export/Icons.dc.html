Pencil lines
on the page

One set, drawn on a 24px grid, one 1.6 stroke, round caps. Every glyph sits in a 48px touch target. They take currentColor, so the same SVG works on paper and on the night sky. Files: ds/icons/kf-*.svg.

The spec
gridviewBox="0 0 24 24", drawn inside a 2px safe zone (live area 20×20).
stroke1.6 at 24px. At 20px (inline) and 16px (chips) it stays 1.6 — scale the box, not the pen.
endsround caps and joins. fill="none" — the star is the one glyph allowed a fill (Top 3 on).
target48 × 48 always (--touch-min). The visual is 24; the hit area never shrinks with it.
colour--ink-muted at rest · --ink-body active · --ink-faint inactive tab · --acc-terra-ink destructive. All ≥3:1 on every paper, day and night.
toneBotanical where it earns it — Today is a sprout, Review a fern crozier, Routines a loop with a leaf, Tomorrow a sun on the horizon. Everything else is plain geometry.
