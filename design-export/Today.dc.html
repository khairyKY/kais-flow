Review notes — what changed vs 1a/1b
  1. Stats said three times. Done-count, streak and inbox appeared in the terrarium band, sidebar and badges. Now each lives in one place.
  2. Date said three times (top bar, eyebrow, H1). Now it's only the H1, with a one-line summary under it.
  3. "Now" was buried in a list halfway down. It's now a card at the top with time left, a Start focus button and a day ribbon. That replaces the decorative terrarium band.
  4. Rituals were duplicated in pinned cards and the right rail. Now they're only in the rail, with Evening collapsed until 7 PM.
  5. Too many taped/tilted cards. Only the goal card keeps tape and tilt, so it's clearly the hero.
  6. Contradiction: "Forecasting · 5 days untouched" appeared right next to today's Forecasting goal. The nudge now points at a project that's actually stale, and "reviewed" became two real actions.
  7. Legibility. The smallest text is now 10.5px (was 8–9px). Metadata moved off --ink-hairline/--ink-faint to --ink-muted to meet 4.5:1 contrast.
  8. Capture was voice-only. Added a typed quick-add field, with the mic next to it. On mobile it sits above the tab bar, and the mic is no longer the middle tab.
  9. Mobile hit targets are now 44px or more: rows 52px, checkboxes 22px, tab labels 10.5px. Stars on Top-3 rows were removed as redundant.
  10. "All open · 14" only showed 3 items and had no way to see the rest. Added "Show 11 more".
  11. The iPhone screen now fills out. Added "Up next" (2 events) and a one-line "Worth a look" nudge. The Now card no longer repeats the next event.
  12. Time-aware (2c). After 7 PM, the Now card becomes the Evening ritual, "Today's three" becomes "How today went" with a "→ Tomorrow" button on anything unfinished, and "Up next" becomes a "Tomorrow" preview.
  13. Tab 5 is called "More". The desktop sidebar now has the full nav (Projects, Journal, People), matching Inbox.
