## 1 · "Duration doesn't sync with the times of scheduling"

**Root cause.** There were several writers and no shared rule:
- The Duration chip (task sheet, popover, editor; `tasks/api setDuration`) changed only `duration_min`. The block never moved. This is item 15's "15m chip doesn't change the duration": the number changed, the block didn't.
- Only `resizeEvent` wrote the block's length back to the task. These paths updated the task's schedule but never its estimate: a drag, the Time sheet's new start and length (`retimeEventWithUndo` → `moveOrResizeEvent`), the block's end field (`updateEvent`), and Plan / Next free slot / Spread / quick create's "holds a task" (`placeTask`).
- A due time never followed its block unless the due date was missed. The date chip printed `due_at`, so item 15's sheet read 5:30 PM for a 5:15–5:30 block.
- In the MCP, `edit_task duration_min` didn't touch the block.

**The rule.** One rule, in `supabase/functions/_shared/blockLength.ts`, shared by the app and the MCP:
- A new length moves the end and keeps the start.
- A new end, or a resize from either edge, is a new length.
- A move keeps the length.
- For a task with a block of its own, `duration_min`, `scheduled_start`/`scheduled_end` and the block stay in step. A due time that was the block's start moves with it.
- An all-day block's days are never the estimate.

**Where it lives.**
- `calendar/api touchTaskSchedule` is the one place every task-block write lands. `resizeEvent`, `linkTaskWithUndo` and `restoreEvent` now go through it too.
- `lengthenTaskBlock` handles the Duration chip. Its Undo puts the block back, and `editWithUndo` now takes a setter's extra Undo.
- What a task shows reads its block. `taskWhen` gives the date chip; `taskMinutes` gives the duration chip, the list row, the rail card, the task menu meta and the sheet meta. A Time slot's times never count as its tasks' lengths.
- Time slots and events already derived their length from their times. The harness confirms the block popover's and the phone sheet's length now match.
- MCP: `edit_task duration_min` stretches the latest timed block. `reschedule_task` keeps the block's length and the estimate follows it.

**Tests first.**
- `calendar/blockLength.test.ts`: the rule itself.
- `calendar/blockLength.flow.test.ts`: 16 paths through the real writes; 7 failed before the fix.
- `settings/mcp.test.ts`: `edit_task` and `reschedule_task`.

**Left for the typing builder (items 15/16).** I didn't fold the duplicate length controls into one; the UI is unchanged apart from what the chips read.

What fails is the phone capture sheet the + opens. A toast over a sheet with a footer docks 8px above that footer, and the capture sheet's field sits right on its footer. Other routes:

- The desktop bar is a centred modal, so toasts never overlap it.
