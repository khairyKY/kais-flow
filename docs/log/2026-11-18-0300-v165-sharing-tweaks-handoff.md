# v1.6.5 sharing tweaks: two switches, her colour, pause with no end (branch claude/v165-sharing-tweaks, from claude/kai-bugs-1012)

Kai's three changes to P1, decided 2026-11-18, plus the colour reaching both her-day widgets. Not merged, not released. Migration **0105** (0101-0103 are held by the other P2 builders; 0104 merged). Merged `origin/claude/kai-bugs-1012` in with `-X ignore-cr-at-eol` (brings in the 0104 widgets).

## What changed
1. **Two switches.** "Show her lane on my calendar" (`circle_members.show_lane`, as before) and "Show her day on Today" (new `show_day`). Each decides only its own surface: the lane chip and `useLanes` read `show_lane`; `HerDayCards` reads `show_day` (P1's "a Partner always shows the card" is gone). Defaults at connection time: both on for Partner, both off for Family/Friend (`share_invite_accept`). Per connection, per viewer, on the viewer's own member row; neither touches what `shared_day` returns. Backfill keeps what P1 drew.
2. **Her colour.** `circle_members.color`, default Heather `#8972B3`, check = one of palette C's 16 day hexes. Picker on the person's page (`PlaceSwatches`, the 16 swatches). Drawn through the swatch's own tokens as `--her` / `--her-ink` (`herVars`): her lane (desktop FullCalendar background events via `eventDidMount`, phone `.pc-lane`), her card, the avatar everywhere. No new colour tokens (`HER_COLOR` is read from `BLOCK_SWATCHES`).
3. **Pause has no automatic end.** "Pause until I turn it back on" (`paused_until = 'infinity'`, already supported by the server) is first; "Pause for an hour" is the secondary timed one; "For today" is gone. Offered in Settings (person page), her card's menu, and the lane's menu (now one tap each, no detour to the person page; it says Resume while paused). New toast "Paused until you turn it back on."
4. **Widgets (coordinator's addition).** `share_widget_read` (0104) is replaced in 0105 to return the colour the key's HOLDER gave that person. `share-widget` feed: `color` (swatch name) on the feed and on every block, and on the paused answer. Android: snapshot `partner.color`; `HerDay.java` uses per-swatch drawables `kfw_blk_her_<swatch>` (dashed edge) and `kfw_circle_her_<swatch>` generated by `docs/log/assets/widgets-build/palette.mjs` (the old hand-made Heather pair removed; the initial is now the swatch tint with its ink, as the app's avatar, so a light swatch stays readable). Rainmeter `herday.lua`: 16-row swatch table (day and night, from the tokens) replacing the Heather constant; `.rmskin` 1.2 rebuilt (unreleased, same version).

## Copy
