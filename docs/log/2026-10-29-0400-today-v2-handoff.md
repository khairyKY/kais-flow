## What changed

**1. Plan my day is back, in the Now card's place, while nothing is planned.**
- The definition is v1.5.0's, reused as is: `topCard(day.state.phase, nowS?.kind, empty) === 'plan'`. In words:
  - the morning ritual is not finished today;
  - the Top 3 doesn't count as planned yet (`dayPhase isPlanned`: at least one pick and 12:00 or later, or every pick done);
  - it is before 17:00, and the morning prompt is pinned;
  - nothing is running, over, paused or just done (any of those wins, as the slip did in v1.5.0).
- Blocks on the calendar don't count as "planned" in that rule. The brief guessed "no blocks today"; v1.5.0 never looked at blocks, so neither does this. A running block still beats the card.
- An empty day keeps its empty state (as in v1.5.0) and gets no card.
- It is v1.5.0's `RitualCard` (Morning · not planned, Plan my day, ~3 min · N in inbox · N overdue, Plan; Resume with its hairline part-way), with a new `panel` prop. The prop draws it in the top panel's paper, line, radius and shadow (`.tp-ritual.is-panel`).
- It also takes the Day bar's place when the device is set to Day.
- Rituals · Begin stays.

**2. The Now · Day switch moved to Settings → Appearance.**
- It is now a "Phone home shows: Now / Day" row, phone widths only, tagged **This device**. The storage is the same (`localStorage['kf-phone-today-view']`, read via `phoneView.ts`).
- There is no Today/Home category in `categories.ts`, so it lives in Appearance. Desktop has no such row.
- The Yesterday / Tomorrow line's middle is the date picker icon again, and the date title still opens the picker. `ViewSwitch` is gone.

**3. iPhone notch.**
- claude/ios-pwa adds `padding-top: var(--sat)` on `.app-shell > main`, which covers everything on Today. This branch adds nothing fixed or sticky at the top, so it needs no inset of its own.
- claude/ios-pwa was **not pushed** by the time I finished (origin has no such branch; its worktree has uncommitted work). So the kai-bugs merge and the WebKit iPhone 15 run are **not done**. To do them after it lands, in this order:

  3. Start the server with `VITE_SUPABASE_URL=http://127.0.0.1:59999`.
  4. Check that the Plan card and the header clear the notch.
- Expected conflict: ios-pwa's touch-area rule in today.css names `.tp-daynav .tp-view .kf-seg-btn`. That selector is dead here; drop it in the merge.

**4. Over warning, option D (Kai's pick; `design-export/Over warning options.html`).** It sits on the Top 3 panel's title.
- **What it counts:** v1.5.0's workload rule, now `todayLayout dayLoad` (`overCapacity` uses it):
  - **planned** = the open Top 3 (each one's block today, else its duration) plus today's other blocks, inside what's left of 9 AM–6 PM;
  - **free** = what's left of that window;
  - **over** = planned minus free.
- **Normal day:** "Top 3 · 6h 20m planned · 7h 20m free", in the panel title's own colours. The line can't wrap: if it doesn't fit, the free part is the first to go. It fits whole at 360 / 390 / 430; squeezed to 250 px, it drops the free part.
- **Over day:** the planned figure turns `--acc-terra-ink`, and a chip under the title reads "1h 50m more than today can fit". The chip uses "Ran over"'s look: palette C's terra edge and fill, `--blk-ran-ink` text. No colour added.
- **Tap the title** (a chevron turns):
  - a line explains the count: "Top 3 and today's blocks, against the free time left today (9:00 AM–6:00 PM). Move a pick to tomorrow to make room."
  - Top 3 rows #2 and #3 show a **Tomorrow** pill in place of ⋯. The pill does the same as the row's own Tomorrow (`moveToTomorrowWithUndo`, with Undo; the pick leaves the Top 3).
  - Tap again to close.
- **Hidden** when the day isn't in its "now" phase: nothing planned (the Plan card shows), or the evening. The title then keeps its plain "· 3h 50m planned" note, as before.
- The title also shows when only the goal is picked but the day is over, so the chip isn't lost.
- It shows in both views, since the Top 3 panel is shared.
- The goal card gets no Tomorrow pill; D drew it only on the rows.

**5. No label chips on phone Today rows** (Kai): one fact per row, as B drew it. Desktop rows and Tasks keep their chips.
