## What it is
- **Server (migration 0100).** circles, circle_members, share_grants (an ordered grant per pair, so N people is more rows), share_invites, share_attempts, share_links, share_pings, plus `private` on tasks and calendar_events. Full note in docs/DATA_MODEL.md (0100).
- **No RLS policy lets a viewer read another person's rows.** A viewer reads only `shared_day(owner, from, to)`; it checks the connection, the lower of the two levels and the pause, then calls `share_day_data()` (no app role can call it), which returns masked columns: no title and no kind at Busy or for anything Private (itself, its task, its series, a task inside a Private Time block). Journal, health, people and chat are not in the code; sharing.sh greps every sharing function body for any table beyond calendar_events, tasks and app_settings (name, zone).
- **Codes.** 8 characters (A-Z, 2-9, no 0/O/1/I), one use, 24 h, 5 waiting at most; 5 wrong tries = 15 min; every bad code reads the same. Peek (the confirm step), then accept.
- **View link.** Function `share-view` (verify_jwt false, in release.yml FUNCTIONS): kf_v_ token, SHA-256 only, Busy or Titles, today + 7 days, 30 days (clamped on every renew), 120 calls/hour, 401/410/429. Page at /v/<token>.
- **Client** (app/src/features/sharing): Settings > Sharing, the invite sheet (8 tiles, message with code and https link), enter-a-code and confirm, a person's page (relationship, level list with previews, the rule line, pause chips, show on my calendar, Disconnect behind a confirm sheet), Her day card on Today after Top 3, the thin Heather lane (desktop week/day as background events, phone day as a bar) with an avatar chip, Private in the block menu/sheet and a Private chip on tasks, People page entry, /join/<code> (code kept through sign-in), kaisflow://join/<code> in tray/deepLink.
- **Own data via the outbox:** my level (my own share_grants row) and the Private flags. Making/accepting codes, pause, leave and the lane switch are online-only.
- **Realtime (my choice).** The only sharing table in the publication is `share_pings`: one row per user holding a timestamp, bumped by triggers after a write the other side could see (not when an item is and stays Private, not while paused) and by a level, pause or leave. The message carries no id, title or time. The client re-asks the masked RPC. A broadcast of row data, or postgres_changes on tasks/events, would have carried raw rows; broadcast channels also need policies on realtime.messages.
- **Legal.** Privacy §5a "Sharing with people you connect" (+ view links), a Sharing line in the Terms (site/src/pages/info.mjs, app/public/privacy.html regenerated), and an informational notice 2026-11-17 in legal.ts (as 0093). **No version bump, no re-consent.** Flag for Kai: the Terms line changes the Terms text without a Terms version bump or a terms notice; decide whether that needs a bump.

2. Her colour is fixed Heather; the design's "Her colour" row is left out.
3. Pause "for today" ends tomorrow 8:00 on the pauser's clock; "until I turn it back on" is supported.
