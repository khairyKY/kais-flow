- **Compost no longer leaks journal photos.**
  - The hourly `capture-image` sweep also removes `journal/<uid>/<entry>/*` photos whose entry row is gone. A trashed entry keeps its photos.

  - Lookups are per user; any error stops before another delete.
  - The conductor reviewed the path layout (`journalPhotoPath`) and the `media_paths` writers: no other entity points into the bucket.
- **A photo-only entry counts as written:** Day N, the week's kept days, the Kept count. Focus's fern says "N photos tonight".
