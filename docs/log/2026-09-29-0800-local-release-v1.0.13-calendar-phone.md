# v1.0.13 — Wave N: Calendar on the phone (touch), the last Wave N screen

Rollback point: `197c4aa` (v1.0.12 on master).

**What's in it (branch `claude/wave-n3` = master + `claude/calendar-phone` + one harness update):**
- **A new phone calendar** (`PhoneCalendar`, under 768px) with its own touch grid. FullCalendar stays on desktop only, and the dead phone branches in CalendarPage / CalendarGrid / QuickCreate / gridClock are deleted.
- **Views and navigation:**
  - 7a: day view with the week strip, unscheduled strip and now line;
  - 7b: swipe to change day;
  - 7h: 3-day view;
  - 7n: tap the title for Day / 3 days / Week.
- **Tapping:**
  - 7c: an empty slot opens the quick-create sheet;
  - 7d: a block opens its block sheet (Time · Open the task · Remind · Unschedule · Delete + Undo);
  - 7g: an unscheduled chip opens a Schedule sheet with the next 3 free slots, or the next days if nothing fits.
- **Moving blocks:**
  - 7e: hold 400ms to lift a block and drag it, with the time bubble, snapping to 15 minutes;
  - 7f: resize from handles outside the block's edges, each with its own 48px target;
  - 7m: the drop shows "Moved to 16:15 · Undo".
- **States and night:** 7i empty day with Plan my day, 7j loading, 7k offline, and night 7l-a / 7l-d.
- **Harness:** the task-sheet harness's from-calendar step now goes through the new block sheet.

**Open for Kai:**

- **Rituals and routines have no sage hatch on the calendar**, because events carry no routine link yet.
- **Choices the drawings didn't settle:**
  - blocks are 15 minutes minimum;
  - the phone grid is in Cairo time while desktop is in device time;
  - all-day events are a chip row above the hours.

**Risks:**

- Dragging doesn't auto-scroll at the grid edge.
- Nothing here has been felt on a real phone (long-press, haptics, keyboard inset, Back).
