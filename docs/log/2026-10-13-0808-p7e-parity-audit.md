# P7e parity audit: can Kai cancel Akiflow?

## 1. Verdict

**Not yet. What stands in the way is three things Kai's own Akiflow week depends on, not the task core.** The daily verbs are at or above Akiflow's speed. Measured on the real app against a mock backend (`docs/log/assets/p7e-audit/verify.mjs`, 18/18 checks pass):

| Verb | Kai's Flow | Akiflow |
|---|---|---|
| Capture (`n` or ⌘K, type, Enter) | 2 keys. A typed time also puts the task on the calendar, and a toast says where it went. | 2 keys |
| Triage | 1 key per inbox item | 1 key per item |
| Move to tomorrow | 2 keys, or 1 swipe on the phone | ~3 keys |
| Time-block | 1 drag on the Calendar page | 1 drag |
| Plan the day | 2–6 taps | a 5–10 minute ritual |

The three blockers come from the vault, which records how Kai actually uses Akiflow (CRITICAL.md, "Akiflow conventions"; Week Plan — CONTEXT):

1. **His five prayer times can't appear.** They are the only fixed anchors of his day, and they come from a read-only calendar that Akiflow shows. Kai's Flow can't show any outside calendar: Google sync was never built, and Settings says "Coming soon".
2. **Time Slots have no equivalent.** Kai uses focus blocks that hold tasks, such as `Code block💻` and `Shaheen🛞`. Kai's Flow has only a plain "Time block" with nothing inside it.
3. **The MCP server can't build a week.** His AI sessions plan his week through Akiflow's MCP server (events, time slots, tasks at a time). Kai's Flow's MCP can add a task with a date-time and move one to tomorrow. It can't create an event or a block, reschedule to a time, or edit a task.

(1) and (2) need a decision from Kai (B1, B2 below). (3) can be built without one (A1). Everything else is either built, cut on purpose, or a small buildable gap (list A). The biggest of those are:

- no Ctrl+Z;
- no calendar view keys;
- "every week" typed in the bar is ignored;
- a timed inbox card is filed without its calendar block.

The last step isn't code. Kai has not yet run a real week in Kai's Flow, and his Akiflow data since the 2026-07-19 import needs a fresh dump and a re-import (the importer is a no-op on duplicates). The honest bar for cancelling is one full week in which nothing sends him back to Akiflow.

### 2.10 Taps and keys for the core Akiflow verbs

Akiflow figures are from AKIFLOW-WIKI §2.1–§2.3 and §2.6. The wiki gives keys, not tap counts, for scheduling, and minutes, not taps, for the planning ritual.

**Capture**
- Akiflow: ⌘E (anywhere) or ⌘K → type → Enter. **2 keys.**
- Desktop: `n` or Ctrl+K → type → Enter. **2 keys** (**p7e c1**). A typed time also time-blocks it. In-app only.
- Phone: **+** → type → **Add**: **2 taps** (**p7e p1**). Hold-to-talk: **1 gesture**.
- Note: "every …" and `//` are not parsed (p7e c2, c3).

**Schedule a task**
- Akiflow: P → date → Enter. **~3 keys.**
- Desktop:
  - j then 1/2/3 (Today / Tomorrow / Next week): **2 keys** (**p7e s1**).
  - Right-click → Tomorrow: **2 clicks**.
  - Any day and time: right-click → Plan… → Pick date & time… → 16 → Set time → 15:00 → Done = **7 clicks** (**p7e s2**). No key opens Plan….
- Phone: swipe right = Tomorrow, **1** (gestures). ⋯ → Plan… → option: **3**.

**Triage the inbox**
- Akiflow: **1 key per item** (E done · P plan · ⌘S someday).
- Desktop: **1 key per item** after one `j`. 3 items = `j e e d` (**p7e t1**).
  - `e` files with the AI's date and place.
  - No "done now".
  - "Plan" means editing the card's date first.
  - The timed card got no calendar block.
- Phone: tap File / Dismiss per card. Hold to select, then bulk.

**Time-block**
- Akiflow: **1 drag** onto the always-visible calendar.
- Desktop, on Calendar: **1 drag** from the rail (**p7e b1**), plus 1 click if the rail is folded (remembered). Plan ▾ → Next free slot: **2 clicks** (**p7e b2**). A typed time at capture: 0 extra.
- Phone: Plan → slot sheet, about 2–3 taps. No drag from a list.

**Plan the day**
- Akiflow: a wizard of about 5–10 minutes (review → 1–3 goals → clear inbox → block).
- Desktop and phone: **2 taps** on a seeded, clean morning; **~6** on a typical one; **~15** at worst. Counted 2026-10-12 §3; MorningRitual hasn't changed since. Covered by rituals "Start the day".

**Done**
- Akiflow: E, **1 key**.
- Desktop: j, e = **2 keys** (**p7e u1**), or 1 click.
- Phone: **1 tap**.

**Undo**
- Akiflow: ⌘Z.
- Desktop: the toast's Undo button only. Ctrl+Z does nothing (**p7e u2**).
- Phone: the toast.

**Reading:** capture, triage, preset scheduling and time-blocking match Akiflow's counts. Kai's Flow is behind where Akiflow is keyboard-only: an arbitrary date/time (7 clicks vs ~3 keys), undo, and the calendar keys.
