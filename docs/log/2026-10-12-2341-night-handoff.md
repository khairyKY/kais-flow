All values are new **night-only tokens** in `styles/tokens/colors.dark.css`: frost ×3, `--glow-hairline`,
`--glow-now`, `--glow-now-terra`, `--silhouette`, `--rim-*` ×5, `--moonflower-*` ×3. The frame's own
values are used where the DS has no token. The fireflies use the DS tokens rather than 1a's
pre-refresh `#E8D9A0`. Day surfaces read these tokens through the fallback pattern, so day never sees
them. `night.css` holds no literal colours and no blurred shadows that aren't tokens (ds:check 0 new).

**Where the moonflower lives, and why.** 1a/1f put it in the terrarium band. Today 2a (desktop) and
Today Phone 2a later removed that band ("replaces the decorative terrarium band"), and putting a
band back would drift from the newer design. In the phone header it would cut "Wednesday, Sep 30" off:
the title is 209 px in a 226 px slot. The band's job, the evening moment with its "Close the garden"
button, now lives in the evening card, so the moonflower opens there. This also keeps 1a's rule
literally: someone on Dark in the morning sees the sun glyph, never the moonflower.

## Not built, and why (with measurements)

1. **`backdrop-filter: blur(6px)` on the frosted cards** → I used the design's flat fill instead. The
   ground behind every frosted card is flat paper, the grain overlay sits *above* the cards, and the
   fireflies fly in front of them. So the blur has nothing to blur. On Settings at 1280×800, blur vs flat
   changed 13,545 px (1.3%) by at most 6/255, which no one can see
   (`night-desktop-settings-blur.png` vs `-flat.png`). Headless scroll traces show no main-thread cost
   either way (0 long tasks, 0 frames over 25 ms in every run). The GPU numbers in headless are
   software-composited and noisy (`trace-summary.md`), so they prove nothing for a real phone, where
   backdrop-filter on big scrolling layers is a known cost. That makes the trade a known risk for zero
   visible gain. The `ponytail:` comment in `night.css` says when to add it back: when something
   moves behind a card.
2. **The terrarium band itself** (1a/1f: plant row, "the moonflower only opens after dark ✿", the counts)
   and the **"Close the garden" button**. 2a replaced both: the counts are in the summary line, and the
   evening card's "Shut down the day · Begin / Shut down" is that action. Copy left as 2a has it.
3. **Sidebar: frosted active row and glowing nav dots (1a).** They are inline styles in
   `components/AppLayout.tsx`, which is outside my files. On the row it is a change of about 4 RGB levels.
4. **Header plant silhouettes on Tasks (cherry, 1b), Calendar (daisy, 1c), Settings (clover, 1e).** They
   are inline styles in features I don't own. The tokens are ready: each is a one-line follow-up,
   `filter: 'var(--rim-blossom | --rim-lavender | --rim-clover, var(--shadow-drop-sm))'`.
5. **1b command bar: the scrim and the dimmed page behind it.** That is the command-bar feature. Its
   panel is already frosted (`blur(8px)`) and renders as drawn (`side-1b.jpg`).
6. **1e copy and behaviour**: the help line "fireflies live on Today only · everything else holds still"
   and "night follows Cairo sunset". Auto still follows the OS (DRIFT-AUDIT §9). That is Settings, and
   a behaviour change, so it is for Kai.
7. The **dusk-veil sweep** at the Auto crossfade is described in prose only, with no frame, so I didn't
   build it.
