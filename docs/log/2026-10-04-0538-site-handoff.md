## What it is

The site is plain static HTML and CSS with about 150 lines of vanilla JS.
- `node build.mjs` (in `site/`) renders 30 pages into `site/dist/`. It needs no `npm install`; Node is enough. It also writes a sitemap, `robots.txt` and an RSS feed of the field notes.
- There's no framework, tracking or cookies. `localStorage` holds only the sun/moon choice and, for one hour, the latest version number.
- Fonts come from Google Fonts: Source Serif 4, Inter Tight, Courier Prime and Caveat, plus Noto Naskh Arabic and IBM Plex Sans Arabic on `/ar/`.
- The tokens are copied, not imported, from `app/src/styles/tokens`: the 59 variables the designs use, day and Night Garden.
- The botanical art comes from `app/public/ds/assets`. The 19 pieces used are now WebP: 371 KB in total, down from 2.9 MB as PNG.
- The app screens in the mockups are lifted verbatim from Kai's design frames into `site/src/screens/*.html`, six of them, and drawn scaled with `transform`. They're real markup, not bitmaps: a description each for screen readers, and they stay English and left-to-right on `/ar/`.
- The share cards, favicons and press zip are rendered by `site/tools/render-assets.mjs` (Playwright and Chrome, run by hand) and committed. That's why the Pages build itself needs only Node.

**Links:**
- "Open the app" goes to https://kais-flow.kaidagoat.workers.dev.
- Downloads go straight to `…/releases/download/<tag>/kais-flow-<tag>.apk` and `…-windows-setup.exe`.
- The tag is v1.0.17 at build time. Each page then asks `api.github.com/…/releases/latest` and swaps in the current tag, date and asset links, cached for an hour. If GitHub doesn't answer, the build-time version stays.

## Kai: deploy (one dashboard step, $0)

1. Cloudflare dashboard → **Workers & Pages → Create → Pages → Connect to Git** → `khairyKY/kais-flow`.
2. Fill in the settings:
   - **Production branch:** `master` (after you merge `claude/site`). Until then, pick `claude/site` to get a preview.
   - **Framework preset:** None.
   - **Root directory:** `site`.
   - **Build command:** `node build.mjs`.
   - **Build output directory:** `dist`.
3. Add the environment variable `SITE_URL` = the site's public address, for example `https://kais-flow-site.pages.dev` or your own domain. Canonical links, share cards, the sitemap and RSS all use it.
4. In **Settings → Builds → Build watch paths**, include `site/*`, so app commits don't rebuild the site.

No `_headers` or `_redirects` are needed. Pages serves `404.html` for unknown addresses by itself. Details are in `site/README.md`.
