# v164-daybar handoff (2026-11-16)

Branch `claude/v164-daybar` (from `origin/claude/kai-bugs-1012`). F1 of the v1.6.4 handoff (`_claude-cloud/kais-flow-v164/HANDOFF-CODEX.md`): on the phone Today's **Day view**, drag a task onto the Day bar to schedule it, swipe the bar to change day. Nothing deployed, no migration, no colour token touched.

## What changed
- **Drag onto the bar** (`features/today/DayBarDrag.tsx`, `barDrop.ts`, `dayBarMath.ts`). A hold (SwipeRow's 400 ms, the same hold + haptic as the calendar) on any task row lifts it: a ghost row follows the finger, the source row dims, and over the bar a vertical line plus a time bubble ("2:15 PM", 12/24h-aware via `clockText`) track the finger, snapped **down** to the quarter hour (14:20 -> 14:15), 7:00 to 22:45. A Time block pill under the finger is ringed in its own hue. Release on the bar calls the calendar's own `dropOnGrid` (into a Time block, else a block of `task.duration_min || 30`, one `scheduleTaskWithUndo` toast + Undo, Ctrl+Z history). Release anywhere else: no write, the ghost eases back to its row (200 ms; instant under reduced motion / Effects off). The release that dropped swallows its click so it never opens the calendar.
- **Hold wiring** (`features/tasks/rowLift.ts`, `SwipeRow.tsx`, `useRowGrammar.tsx`). `SwipeRow.onLongPress` now receives `{pointerId, x, y, el}`; a new `RowLiftContext` lets a page turn the hold into a lift. `useRowGrammar` reads it, so every row built on the grammar lifts: Top 3, goal card, Today's tasks, Overdue / Also open, back-from-snooze, and the other days' rows. Done rows never lift. While a selection is under way a hold still selects.
- **Swipe the bar** (`DayStripView.tsx` `useBarSwipe`). Touch only, axis-locked (`lockAxis`); the bar follows the finger, >= 48 px or a flick (`FLICK_VELOCITY`) goes `nav.step(+1 / -1)` (left = next day), the bar leaves with `SETTLE_MS` / `--ease-standard` and the next day's bar comes in from the other side; shorter springs back. Reduced motion or Effects off: the day just changes. `.tp-dbar` is `touch-action: pan-y`, so a vertical drag over it still scrolls the page. A tap still opens the calendar column, a pill tap still opens its block (a swipe's release swallows its click). The Previous/Next buttons and Shift+Left/Right are untouched.
- **The bar follows the day shown.** `DayStripView` takes `day` (was always the real today); the now tick and the Next line are today's only. Phone `OtherDay` (`?day=`) now draws the bar too when "Phone home shows" is Day, for past days as well (read-only there: swipe works, no drops), so a swipe never strands you on a day with no bar.
- `TodayView` / `OtherDay` wrap their phone tree in `DayBarDrag` (enabled: phone + Day view + not the Plan-my-day card, and for other days not past). One tree on or off, so toggling never remounts the page.

## Choices the spec didn't cover
- **Not PhoneGrid's `carry`.** It's built around the grid's columns and scroll; the bar needs only x -> minutes, so this is a ~140-line single-purpose drag, sharing `dropOnGrid`, `spanIso`, `slotAt`, `longPress`/`tick`, `LONG_PRESS_MS`, `lockAxis`.
- **Empty days.** Today's own empty day still shows no bar (Kai 2026-11-03), except while a task is lifted (the bar appears so there is somewhere to drop; the rows below shift down by one bar). Other days show it always.
- **Hold no longer selects on the phone Day view.** A hold lifts instead (per the spec). Selection is still reachable from a row's ⋯ menu -> Select, and once something is selected a hold selects more. The Now view is unchanged (hold selects).
- **Ghost rides 68 px above the finger while over the bar** so the line and bubble stay visible.
- The page's boundary-resistance pull (`useBoundaryResistance`) stands down only for a `defaultPrevented` move, so the lift's non-passive `touchmove` is registered in the **capture** phase on `window` (a bubble-phase one ran after it and the page rubber-banded 40 px under the finger; the harness caught it).
- `TodayPage.tsx` and `useRowGrammar.tsx` are CRLF files; endings kept.

## Left over
- Real-device feel (Android WebView / iOS) is unverified: CDP touch is faithful to pointer + touch events but not to the OS gesture arena. Worth a thumb test on the phone: hold-lift timing against a slow scroll, and that Android's long-press contextmenu stays quiet (SwipeRow already swallows it for touch).
- Subtask rows inside a nest and the Now view's Next rows are not lift-aware on purpose (Day view only; the nest has its own row component).
