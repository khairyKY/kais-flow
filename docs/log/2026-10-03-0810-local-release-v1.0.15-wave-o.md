**What's in it (branch `claude/wave-o` = `claude/decisions` + calendar-followups + email + plan-fixes + importers):**
- **Kai's calls, decided on UX grounds:**
  - Next week on a Sunday is the Monday after.
  - A reminder keeps its lead time when its task moves (once, in `writeRow`).
  - Phone routines on Today show the current part of the day; the rest fold into a line.
  - Duplicate and link glyphs.
  - No duplicate date quick picks; an overdue date opens on this month.
- **The pressing ceremony:** no auto-advance (Next / → / ←), and the press really closes.
- **Phone calendar:**
  - tapping a task block opens the Task sheet, which now has Change time and Unschedule;
  - "+N" for 4+ overlapping blocks;
  - auto-scroll while dragging.
- **Plan my day:** search all open tasks and pick them in place, plus "Show all". Time pills are dashed while they're suggestions and solid once you set them; the ✓ is gone, and Start the day places every timed pick.
- **Importers:** Todoist, TickTick, Notion, Obsidian/Markdown, Kindle highlights and Goodreads, plus "Undo this import".
- **Email confirmation screens** (9c–9f, switch on by themselves) and **branded auth email templates.** Confirmation stays **off** until SMTP secrets exist; the release's Auth-email step is a no-op without them.
