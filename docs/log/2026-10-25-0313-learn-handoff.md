## Audit: what exists today

**Onboarding** (`app/src/features/onboarding/*`; First Run 9g/9h):
- **One screen:** an optional name, then "What are 3 things on your mind today?". Each line is read by the command bar's own parser (dates, `30m`, `!!`, labels, a repeat, notes, a link), and every line becomes a Top 3 task.
- **Exits:** Start (disabled until a line has text), Skip, and "Import from Akiflow / CSV instead".
- **After it:** it sets `onboarded_at` and logs `onboarding.completed`. It leaves two flags on that device: the first-Today line ("↑ this card always shows your next move") and "tour pending".
- **Replant:** Settings → Replant reopens it with `?replant`.
- **Harness:** `docs/log/assets/first-run/verify.mjs`, against a mocked backend.

**The tour** (`app/src/features/tour/help.ts`, `TourHost.tsx`):
- **Five garden notes on the first Today**, then a closing card.
  - Phone: capture, Top 3, swipe (with a ghost finger), Plan / Shut down, and Chat under More.
  - Desktop: sidebar, ⌘K, Top 3, the calendar, and the `?` card.
- **Three one-line hints**, met once each: swipe (touch only), calendar, Inbox.
- **"Seen" storage:** kept per user in localStorage and in `app_settings.help_seen`, so a new phone doesn't repeat them.
- **"Show me around again"** sits in the Guide, Settings → App and the `?` sheet.

**The in-app Guide** (`app/src/features/guide/*`, in the name menu / sidebar foot and More):
- **11 articles in 4 sections:** Start here, Your day, Bringing things in, Your data.
- **Each article:** 3 to 6 steps, one line each, with a crop of the real screen (from the tour harness).
- **The index:** search, the tour card, the `?` shortcut sheet, and "Still stuck? Ask in Chat".

**The website** (`site/src/pages/guides.mjs`, `features.mjs`, field notes in `more.mjs`):
- **9 guides:** getting started, import, gestures, keyboard shortcuts, capture from anywhere, connect an AI assistant, Android, Windows, your data.
- **7 feature pages**, plus field notes (the changelog).
- The website and the app overlap: getting started, import, gestures and data are written twice.

**What a new person still needs, to get from zero to a calm first week:**
- **The why.** Every piece teaches a button. Nothing anywhere says "clear the Inbox daily", "three is plenty" or "close the day", or why.
- **Days 2 to 7.** Onboarding ends on day one. Ritual times are never asked for, and focus rounds and the weekly review are never introduced.
- **Guide gaps.** These have no Guide article: the Inbox, Time slots, Focus, Review, Journal, People, Library, Paper, smart lists, snooze, areas and domains, labels, Chat and MCP.
- **The typing marks** live only in the website's shortcut table, never where you type.

## Research: how other apps teach

The full notes, with sources, are in `D:\Coding\_claude-cloud\kais-flow-how-apps-teach-2026-10-23.md`.

- **Sunsama:** the first daily plan is the tutorial, and it repeats lightly on days 2 and 3.
- **Akiflow:** `?` opens a help panel with a setup checklist.
- **Todoist:** a methods hub and a quiz.
- **Things:** a "Meet Things" project that teaches by doing.
- **Notion:** one question, then seeded templates.
- **TickTick:** teaches features, not a method.
- **Superhuman:** game-style self-serve onboarding, which took setup completion from 30% to 98%.

## Checked against the code while drawing

- **What `parseCommand.ts` reads:** chrono dates and times (and `<fri`), `#project` (fuzzy, else a domain), `*label`, `!`/`!!`/`!!!`, `30m`/`1h`/`1h30m`, "every …", `// notes`, the first link, and `>slot`.
  - The `>slot` token **exists**: it names a Time block ahead, by name, as in the queue's "verify this".
  - Esc hands the last token back as words, and ⌘2 makes the line an event.
- **Priority words.** The app names them **Medium / High / Critical** for `!` / `!!` / `!!!` (`PRIORITY_NAME`, `PRIORITY_LABELS`); no mark means no priority. The queue note called `!` "low". The page uses the app's words and asks Kai to say if `!` should read Low. This is a naming decision for the typing builder.
- **Time blocks** are made from quick create: click or drag a gap, then the Task / Event / Time block switcher. ⌘-click a gap makes a task. The Guide drawings say this.
- **The Shut down line** is saved to the journal (`EveningRitual.tsx`, `journal.line_added`), so METHOD.md says "one line in your journal".
- **Focus** rounds default to 25 minutes (`focusStore` `focusRoundMin`). The weekly review is "The sweep", with Slipping, streaks, hours by area, focus trend and blooms.
- **Onboarding C / method C feasibility:** the seven checklist items map to events already in the activity log, so the card needs no new writes.
