## Step 2: phone drag (built)

- A hold on a task row inside a slot lifts **that task**, not the slot. The task follows the finger with 7e's lift look (1.04, +1.2°, the popover shadow). Its place in the slot stays as a dashed outline, and the row takes the long-press bloom (Effects 2l). It ticks once per quarter.
- When you let go:
  - On empty time it gets its own block there (the dashed ghost and the "12:30–13:15" bubble show it first).
  - On another slot it goes into that slot (the slot is ringed while the task is over it).
  - On the strip, or off the grid, it comes out of the slot.
  - Back on its own slot, nothing happens.
  - Each has Undo. These are the desktop's rules, written once as `slots.heldDrop` with a unit test.
- A hold on the slot's title or empty area still lifts the slot.
- Files: `PhoneGrid.tsx` (the `task` gesture, `heldAt`/`trackHeld`, the edge scroll only over the grid), `PhoneCalendar.tsx` (`dropHeld`), `phoneCalendar.css`, `slots.ts` + test.
