# Tour & help: garden notes, one-line hints, the Guide

Branch `claude/tour`, cut from `origin/claude/wave-u` (v1.0.22 + calendar rail + sounds), with wave-u merged back in twice (tasks-noise, the Arabic-layout hotkeys fix, capture-block). Nothing is merged into wave-u, released or deployed.

A friend testing the app looked for a tutorial and didn't find one. This adds three layers over the existing screens, built from Kai's three exports.

## What's in it

**1 · The first-run tour** (`app/src/features/tour/`).
- **When it runs.** Once, on the first Today after onboarding. Onboarding's Start, Skip or Import leaves `kf-tour-pending:<uid>`; Replant doesn't.
- **Phone, 14a–14f.** Five garden notes on the real Today, then the closing card:
  1. Capture: "Tap to write, hold to talk"
  2. Top 3
  3. A task row, with a looping ghost-finger swipe
  4. Plan my day / Shut down
  5. Ask (the More tab)
  6. Closing card: "That's the garden…", with Done
- **Desktop, 14g.**
  1. The sidebar
  2. The Capture button (Ctrl+K / ⌘K)
  3. Top 3
  4. The calendar (the tour goes to `/calendar`)
  5. The "? shows every shortcut" card, with Done
- **Controls.** Every note has Next and Skip and a seedling progress. Skip and Done both count as seen. Escape and Android Back skip.
- **Anchors.** Notes anchor to `data-tour="…"` attributes, one per element:
  - `capture`, `top3` (×2), `task-row` (Today + Tasks), `plan` (DayCard + RitualCard), `plan-menu` (Today's ⋯), `more`
  - `sidebar`, `command-bar`, `calendar` / `calendar-block` (desktop + phone), `inbox-item`
- **A missing anchor skips its note** after 2.5 s. The tour always has an end.
  - On an empty day the phone gets capture → plan (on the ⋯ menu) → ask → done.
- **It never shows over sign-in or onboarding.** It lives in the signed-in shell.
- **It waits while a sheet, menu or dialog is up:** anything on the escape stack (`lib/overlayStack.overlayCount`) or any `[aria-modal]`.
- **The dim is 24% and takes no taps.** Only the card does, so "tap the lit thing to try it" works.
- **Reduced motion:** a still ↔ arrow replaces the ghost loop.

**2 · Just-in-time hints (14h).** Each is gold paper with a seedling, one Caveat line and ×.
- The three hints:
  - The swipe hint, on the first task row in view. Touch only.
  - The first calendar visit: "Hold a block to move it, drag its edges to resize". On desktop it reads "Drag a block to move it; pull an edge to resize."
  - The first Inbox with items: "Each one becomes a task, a note, or nothing."
- **When.** After the page settles (1.5 s). One at a time, never during the tour.
- **Once each.** A hint counts as seen the moment it shows.
- **Gone** on ×, on touching any of its kind (a swipe, a held block, filing), on leaving the page, or when a sheet opens.
- **The tour teaches them too.** Its row note marks the swipe hint seen, and its calendar note marks the calendar hint seen.

**3 · Remembered per user.**
- **On this device:** localStorage `kf-help:<uid>`.
- **On the account:** `app_settings.help_seen` (**migration 0058**, `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` updated).
- **Seen** means the union of the two.
- **Before 0058 is pushed** the client never sends the column. It writes only when the fetched row already has the key, so nothing dead-letters. Until the push, seen-state is per device.
- **Every write goes through the outbox:** `updateAppSetting` → `writeRow`.

**4 · The Guide** (`/guide`, `/guide/:slug`; its own lazy chunk).
- **Where.** A Guide tile in the phone's More sheet. A Guide row in the sidebar foot between Chat and Settings.
- **The index.** Search, then "Show me around again", then 11 articles in 4 sections. Desktop also has the "? shows every shortcut · Still stuck? Ask in Chat" box.
- **Articles.** Each has 3–6 steps, each step a line plus a real crop.
  - The desktop article has "On this page".
  - The phone article ends with the tour card and "Next: …".
- **The copy is from the app as it is now:**
  - **Capture:** tap = type, hold = talk. The AI fills date / priority / length / notes. `!` `!!` `!!!` · `30m` · `#project` · `*label`. A typed time also makes a block (capture-block).
  - **Calendar:** a time puts a task on the calendar; a date alone keeps it in that day's list. Plus the Overdue · Today · Inbox rail, 15-minute snaps and hold-to-lift on the phone.
  - **Getting started:** What's new, Settings → App → Check for updates, and Sounds (Felt / Kalimba / Glass).
  - **Your data:** Groq, offline, the sign-out prompt, Trash 30 days.
- **The crops.**
  - 53 WebP files in `app/public/guide/`, **381 KB in total**, lazy-loaded, never in a JS chunk.
  - `crops.json` gives each one's size, so nothing jumps.
  - Made by `docs/log/assets/tour/crops.mjs` from the mock app: Playwright clips at 1.5×, WebP from Chrome's own canvas encoder (no new dependency).
- **"Show me around again"** sits in the Guide, Settings → App (under Check for updates) and the ? sheet. It clears every seen key and starts the tour on Today.

**5 · The chat's empty state (14l).**
- The awake clover, "Ask anything about your day.", and "It knows your tasks, calendar and routines."
- Three starters, each 52 px tall; a tap sends it: "What's on today?", "What should I drop?", "How do I move a task to tomorrow?".
- The placeholder now reads "Ask about your day…".

**6 · The ? sheet (14k-3)** gets the paper treatment.
- A solid taped sheet with a fern, a ×, "Shortcuts", and "the quick paths through the garden".
- It's now `role=dialog aria-modal`.
- Its foot reads "On a Mac it's ⌘; on Windows, Ctrl." with "Show me around again" and "Open the Guide →".
- The keys listed are unchanged, because they're the ones that work.

## Deliberate differences from the exports

1. **The hints float.** They sit under (or over) the first thing of their kind and cover the row below until dismissed. The export (14h) inserts the swipe hint between two rows. Inserting it would mean editing the list code other builders are in.
2. **No separate Plan-my-day hint.** The ritual's Suggested times step already says "Times are suggestions — tap one to change it. Start the day puts them on your calendar." every time (the app wins).
3. **Note 4 says "After five"**, not "six". The app's evening starts at 17:00 (`dayPhase.SHUTDOWN_FROM`).
4. **The desktop ⌘K note points at the Capture button on Today**, the bar's closed face. The export shows the open bar with sample text. The tour never shows over a modal, and an open bar under a tour invites a stray Enter.
   - The line reads "Ctrl+K" on Windows.
   - The sub-line says "the date and the details are filled in", because the AI fills date, priority and notes, not a person.
5. **The ghost swipe animates a copy of the lit row** laid over it. The real row is never touched; touching it stops the ghost. Its underlay reads "Delete", the app's own swipe label, not the export's "Trash".
6. **More stays the app's grid sheet with a Guide tile.** The export draws a list with a "How things work · Show me around again" sub-line. The sidebar's Guide row has no active highlight, matching Settings beside it.
7. **No "Was this helpful? Yes / Not really".** There's nowhere $0 to send it.
8. **"Your data & privacy" reads "What's stored where, and who reads it"**, not "…how to export". The app has no export.
9. **Step counts:**
   - Getting started has 6 steps (the brief adds What's new, Check for updates and Sounds).
   - Calendar has 5.
   - Data & privacy has 5.
10. **Crops are day-theme only** (to stay at 381 KB), so a night article shows day crops. The desktop article's crop column is 300 px.
11. **No Arabic layer exists.** The page is English, built with logical properties; the RTL frame (`phone-day-guide-rtl.webp`) shows the Guide mirroring.
12. **The ? sheet lists only keys that exist:** Anywhere · On a task · Inbox triage · Command bar. The export's G-key go-tos and calendar keys aren't bound.
13. **The chat's header and composer are unchanged**, apart from the placeholder.
14. **Night dims at 40%**, not 24%, so the cut-out still reads on dark paper.
