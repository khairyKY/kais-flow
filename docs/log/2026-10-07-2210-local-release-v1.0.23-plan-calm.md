# v1.0.23 — plan, replan, and a calmer day

Rollback point: `14ca8ff` (v1.0.22 on master).

**Branch `claude/wave-u`** = master (v1.0.22) + `claude/calendar-rail` + `claude/sounds` + `claude/tasks-noise` + `claude/capture-block` + `claude/plan-replan` + `claude/phone-polish` + `claude/tour` + integration fixes.

## What's in it
- **Plan / Replan:**
  - one menu everywhere: Today · Next free slot (ASAP, confirm first) · Tomorrow, first thing · This weekend · Next week · Pick date & time…, each with a hint;
  - Settings → Calendar has **weekend days** and the shortcut glossary;
  - Today's **Overdue · N** group has Replan all.
- **Goal of the day:**
  - "Make goal of the day" in every task menu, plus Plan my day's ☆ Make goal;
  - the Top 3 order is set by drag, Move up/down or Alt+↑/↓;
  - the goal and the order sync across devices (`tasks.top3_rank`, 0055).
- **Calendar:**
  - the rail has Overdue · Today · Inbox (+ folded Lists);
  - the Akiflow rule: a time puts a task on the calendar as a block, a date alone doesn't;
  - a replan moves or clears the old block (the 4am bug);
  - the details panel looks editable;
  - short blocks are readable, tall titles wrap, and the draft no longer shows over sheets;
  - a typed time in a capture makes a block.
- **Tasks:** Ctrl+A on every list (incl. Arabic layouts), Move to… (domain / area / project / none), an area's or project's domain with its tasks following, and a quiet sync strip (no steady "Synced", no change spam).
- **Sounds:** seven event sounds in three packs; **Felt** is the default.
- **Phone polish:** Routines (ritual cards, ⋯ instead of "archive", gutters), every More page audited, a ＋ on the capture button, toasts no longer covering full-screen headers.
- **Tour & Help:** the first-run garden notes, one-time hints, the Guide (11 articles), chat starters, and the paper ? sheet (`app_settings.help_seen`, 0058).

## Integration fixes (this session)
- **Settings:**
  - the Weekend choice takes its own full-width line (it ran past the card at 150%);

- **Tour:**
  - the phone calendar hint docks above the tab bar instead of over the blocks;
  - the tour's scroll-to-target is vertical-only (`scrollIntoView` had scrolled a clipped row sideways).
- **Shortcuts and noise:**
  - Ctrl+K / Ctrl+J / Ctrl+/ work on an Arabic keyboard layout;
  - no routine "completed" pop-up.
- **Harnesses:**
  - gestures + gestures pages follow the new menu;
  - web haptics checks dropped (native-only);
  - the one-time Updated toast is seeded away where it covered tapped rows;
  - the desktop-polish Calendar card has two selected segments;
  - the plan-replan glossary format;
  - projects-fixes names any sideways-scrolled element.
