## Findings the build needs

- **The current block colours fail AA.** Measured on every fill a block's text can sit on (the grid, today's wash, and the "Now" wash):
  - The time row in today's text tones goes down to **2.8:1** (Terra at night; Blossom at night is 3.4).
  - Titles go down to **4.4:1** (Buttercream at night, on the Now wash).
  - **"Ran over" terra text reads 2.2–3.8:1 on every fill**, by day and at night.
- **How A, B and C pass:**
  - Each palette computes its text tone per swatch: the swatch's own hue, stepped darker by day or lighter at night until it clears 4.6:1.
  - Night fills are lighter: 20% for A and C, 18% for B.
  - Each palette gets its own "Ran over" terra: **#7C341D by day and #FFE4D9 at night** (#FFE0D3 for C).
  - Minimum ratios:

    | Palette | Time row | Titles | Ran over |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | A | 4.6 | 4.7 | 4.6 |
    | B | 4.6 | 4.8 | 4.6 |
    | C | 4.6 | 5.0 | 4.7 |

  - Cross-checked in Python with `ds-bound/check_contrast.py`'s own `lum`/`ratio`. The result matched to within 0.04.
- **Night text tones lose most of their hue.** AA on a mid-dark fill needs near-cream text. The terra edge still says "ran over" at night.
- **`ds-bound/check_contrast.py` on the tokens shows 2 failures from before this work**, both at exactly 4.50 (terra-ink and sig-overdue on paper-sidebar). No token changed here.
